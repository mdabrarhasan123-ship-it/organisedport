01 / WHO I AM

I am Abrar Hasan Sanim.

I am a student of Dhaka College, currently studying Business Studies. I am interested in understanding people, ideas, institutions and the world around me. Reading, writing, thinking and exploring different perspectives are some of the things that continuously shape the way I see life.

I am still learning, still experimenting and still creating my path. I do not see myself as someone who has already figured everything out. Rather, I see this stage of life as a time to observe, learn and prepare for what comes next.

Student · Learner · Explorer
02 / ACADEMIC ROOTS

Where the journey began.

My academic journey started long before Dhaka College. The schools, teachers and experiences of those early years played a quiet but important role in shaping the person I am becoming.

Those years were not simply about textbooks and examinations. They were the foundation of my curiosity, discipline and desire to understand things beyond what was immediately visible.

03 / SCHOOL YEARS

Learning to look beyond.

School was a period of gradual change. Alongside academic studies, I began developing a stronger interest in people, society, ideas and the questions that do not always have simple answers.

It was also during these years that I began to understand that education is not limited to what happens inside a classroom.

04 / COLLEGE LIFE

Dhaka College.
A new chapter.

I am currently studying at Dhaka College as a Business Studies student. College has opened a different environment for me—one where academic learning, communication, collaboration and independent thinking meet.

Beyond the classroom, I have become involved in different creative and organisational activities. These experiences continue to teach me how to work with people, take responsibility and turn ideas into something real.
